The fortune tiger martingale strategy applies the familiar idea of raising the stake after a loss.

Fortune Tiger isn't a simple:

A paying spin can return:

Do Fortune Tiger Patterns Work?

A fortune tiger pattern strategy tries to use previous results as a signal for the next bet.

For example:

Loss → Loss → Loss → Increase Bet

or:

Small Win → Small Win → Big Win Expected

These sequences can look surprisingly convincing when you examine old results.

The problem comes when you try to use them before the next result exists.

Three losses describe the previous three spins. They don't create a requirement for the fourth to win.

Similarly, two small wins don't mean a larger multiplier must follow.

This is why patterns are often easier to discover in screenshots than to reproduce consistently in future play.
