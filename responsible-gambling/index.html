Responsible Gambling

Last updated: September 2026

Our approach

Fortune Tiger provides informational content about casino games. Gambling involves financial risk and should never be treated as a source of guaranteed income.

Set limits

Decide your spending and time limits before you play. Do not chase losses or increase stakes because of previous outcomes.

Age and local rules

Use gambling services only if you meet the legal age and other requirements that apply where you live.

Get help

If gambling is affecting your finances, relationships or wellbeing, stop playing and seek support from a recognised local gambling-support organisation.
