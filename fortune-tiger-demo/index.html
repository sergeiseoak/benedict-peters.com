Fortune Tiger Demo and RTP

A short Demo session isn't a reliable way to calculate fortune tiger rtp.

RTP is a theoretical long-term measure built into the mathematical configuration of a game. The results of 50, 100 or even several hundred test spins can differ substantially from that theoretical figure.

One player might finish the first 50 Demo spins with more virtual credits than they started with. Another could lose most of the test balance over the same number of rounds. Neither result, by itself, establishes the game's RTP.

If you want the applicable figure, check the information or paytable section of the active version instead of trying to calculate it from a short run of Demo results.
