Fortune Tiger RTP: What Players Need to Know

RTP describes the theoretical long-term return associated with a particular mathematical configuration of the game.

For understanding how to play fortune tiger, the most important point is that RTP isn't a percentage that Fortune Tiger has to return to your individual balance during a session.
