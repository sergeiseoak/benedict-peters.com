Fortune Tiger RTP: What the Percentage Really Means

Fortune tiger rtp refers to Return to Player, the theoretical long-term percentage associated with a particular mathematical configuration of the game.

It shouldn't be treated as a promise about an individual session. If, for example, you deposit ₦10,000, the RTP doesn't mean that a fixed percentage of that amount will automatically return to your balance before you stop playing. Short-term results can differ considerably from the theoretical figure.

It's also better to check the version of the game you're actually playing rather than relying on an RTP number copied from an old review. Games can be distributed in different mathematical configurations, making the figure shown in the active game's information or paytable the one that matters.
