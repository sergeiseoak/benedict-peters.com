Wagering Requirements Explained With a Naira Example

A wagering requirement tells you how much qualifying play is required under the promotion before bonus funds or related winnings become withdrawable according to the offer's rules.

Suppose the terms say:

Bonus: ₦5,000 Wagering: 20× bonus

The wagering target would be:

₦5,000 × 20 = ₦100,000

That doesn't mean you're required to lose ₦100,000. It means qualifying bets totalling ₦100,000 need to be placed under those promotional rules.

There's still financial risk while completing wagering, and the balance can be lost before the requirement is completed.
